中文（香港）

「賽博蛇」（下稱「本遊戲」）由個人開發者 fung2222 製作。我哋重視你嘅私隱，以下說明本遊戲點樣處理資料。

1. 我哋唔會收集個人資料。本遊戲毋須註冊或登入，亦唔會要求你嘅姓名、電郵、電話、位置、相片、聯絡人或任何個人身份資料。

2. 只喺你部機本地儲存。遊戲會用瀏覽器 / App 嘅本地儲存（localStorage）記低：最高分、遊戲進度、設定（例如靜音）。呢啲資料只會留喺你部裝置，唔會上載去任何伺服器。你可以隨時清除瀏覽器資料或解除安裝 App 嚟刪除。

3. 網頁版冇廣告、冇分析追蹤。網頁版（GitHub Pages）唔會載入任何廣告、分析或第三方追蹤程式。GitHub 作為網站寄存商可能會為保安及營運記錄伺服器日誌（例如 IP 地址），詳情請參閱 GitHub 私隱聲明。

4. 日後 Android App 版本嘅廣告。如本遊戲推出 Google Play Android 版本，可能會使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告（只喺自然停頓位置顯示插頁廣告，以及你自願選擇觀看嘅獎勵廣告）。AdMob 可能會收集及使用裝置識別碼（例如廣告 ID）、IP 地址、大致位置及使用資料，用嚟顯示及衡量廣告、防止詐騙。喺需要嘅地區（例如歐洲經濟區及英國），App 會先透過 Google 用戶同意訊息平台（UMP）徵求你嘅同意，你亦可以喺 App 內隨時更改私隱選項。詳情請參閱 Google 點樣使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式嘅資料。你可以喺 Android「設定 › 私隱 › 廣告」重設或刪除廣告 ID。

5. 兒童。本遊戲嘅目標對象係 13 歲或以上人士，並非專為兒童設計。我哋唔會刻意收集兒童嘅個人資料。

6. 政策更新。如有更改，我哋會更新本頁面及上方生效日期。

7. 聯絡我哋。如有任何查詢，請喺 GitHub Issues 留言。

English

"CYBER SNAKE" (the "Game") is made by independent developer fung2222. This policy explains how the Game handles data.

1. We do not collect personal information. No account or login is required. The Game never asks for your name, email, phone number, location, photos, contacts or any other personally identifying information.

2. Local storage only. The Game uses your browser's / app's local storage (localStorage) to remember your best score, game progress and settings (such as mute). This data stays on your device and is never uploaded to any server. You can delete it at any time by clearing site data or uninstalling the app.

3. No ads or analytics on the web version. The web version (GitHub Pages) loads no advertising, analytics or third-party trackers. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may keep server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Ads in a future Android app. If the Game is released on Google Play, the Android version may show ads through Google AdMob (interstitial ads only at natural breaks, and rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one). AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and usage data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where required (e.g. EEA and UK), the app will ask for your consent first through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), and you can change your privacy choices in the app at any time. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.

5. Children. The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes. We will update this page and the effective date above if this policy changes.

7. Contact. Please open an issue at GitHub Issues.
